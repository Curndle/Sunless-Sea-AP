Sunless Sea AP is my attempt to mod Sunless Sea to be compatible with the Multiworld Randomizer Archipelago 
(Found here https://github.com/ArchipelagoMW/Archipelago)

This is far from being complete as is, but I figured I may as well share my work for now.

The project is split into three part
1) APworld
2) Game Mod
3) BepInEx Connector

Of the three, the APworld is the only one complete, at least as far as a proof of concept release
The Game Mod isn't close to finished, but I understand how I would get it to a functional state at least.
BepInEx Connector is just this template https://github.com/alwaysintreble/ArchipelagoBepInExPluginTemplate
it's not remotely functional currently, it won't do anything in game, and you can't even type your login details.

I don't think I can finish the BepIn Connector myself, and that saps my motivation to work on everything else for now.

Anyone who wants to fork this to work on their own version is free to do so, all I ask is that you credit me in some way, I guess?
Otherwise I'll get back to this at some point, maybe.
